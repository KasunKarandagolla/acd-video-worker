# Genre Playbooks — Football Emotion Variants

Read this as Stage 10, after the general editorial stage has selected/assembled a plan and the agent needs football-specific conventions.

## Football heartbreak

Use restraint. Prioritize final whistle, tunnel walks, crying/blank faces, teammate consolation, crowd silence, commentator restraint, and aftermath. Avoid hype transitions and excessive SFX.

## Football comeback / redemption

The fall must be real. Use injury/doubt/failure footage, then training/grind, then the decisive moment and reaction. The payoff only works if the low point was felt.

## World Cup national pride

Balance individual player close-ups with crowd, flag, team bench, coach, family/fan, and national context. The country is part of the protagonist.

## Iconic skill / genius

Do not make it only a dribbling compilation. Use opponent reaction, commentator disbelief, crowd rise, slow-motion clarity, and legacy framing.

## Underdog shock

Show scale difference first: big opponent, odds/expectation, crowd tension. The payoff must include opponent disbelief and fan explosion.

## Rivalry / revenge

Use evidence, not invented drama. Establish conflict through verified context, then build action-reaction-proof. Avoid toxic or misleading framing.

## Long-form 8-12 minute mode

Long-form needs waves, not one straight rise:

1. cold open emotional preview;
2. context and stakes;
3. first pressure wave;
4. setback/fall;
5. grind/attempts;
6. turning point;
7. peak event;
8. aftermath and meaning;
9. final reflection/coda.

Use interviews, commentary, social/media context, and quieter moments to avoid a 10-minute highlight reel.

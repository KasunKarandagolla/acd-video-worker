# Narrative Structure for Football Emotion Edits

Read this during Stage 1 brief interpretation and Stage 4 sequencing when the agent must choose or repair the emotional arc.

## One emotional question

Every video answers one emotional question. Examples:

- **Legacy/comeback:** Can a player written off as finished complete the one story still missing?
- **Heartbreak:** What does it look like when a final chance disappears?
- **Heroic failure:** Can greatness still hurt when the trophy is lost?
- **Underdog/national pride:** What happens when a whole country believes through one team?
- **Iconic skill:** Why did one moment make everyone feel the player was unstoppable?

If the emotional question is unclear, do not continue to sourcing. Return to `brief-interpretation.md`.

## Core arc phases

Use only the phases that runtime can support:

1. **Cold open:** tease payoff or pain quickly.
2. **Setup:** who/what matters and why.
3. **Fall/pressure:** the real obstacle or emotional low point.
4. **Grind/buildup:** escalating effort, tension, or repeated attempts.
5. **Turning point:** the pivot where belief or failure becomes visible.
6. **Triumph/heartbreak/payoff:** the main emotional event.
7. **Coda:** release, reflection, final face/crowd/trophy/tunnel moment.

## Football story templates

### Pain → pressure → comeback → legacy
Best for Messi 2022, injury returns, doubter-to-proof stories.
Requires visible pressure, decisive action, emotional reaction, and aftermath.

### Hope → final chance → failure → silence
Best for Ronaldo/Morocco-style heartbreak or missed-final moments.
Requires restraint: do not edit this like a hype reel.

### Nation hopes → player burden → decisive moment → collective reaction
Best for World Cup/national pride stories.
Requires crowd/fan/team reaction, not only player action.

### Rivalry → humiliation → revenge/proof
Best when there is a clear antagonist or public doubt.
Requires careful evidence; do not invent conflict.

### Skill impossibility → repetition → crowd disbelief → legacy meaning
Best for iconic dribbling/technical genius edits.
Requires close visual clarity and rhythm; the edit still needs emotional meaning, not just tricks.

## Runtime adaptation

- 15s: hook + payoff + coda only.
- 30s: simplified setup → conflict → payoff.
- 60s: full arc with compressed phases.
- 3min+: sub-arcs possible.
- 8-12min long-form: full context, multiple pressure waves, documentary pacing, silence, interviews/commentary, and stronger source diversity.

## Decision rule

Choose the honest arc. Do not force a triumph ending onto heartbreak, and do not force heartbreak onto a legacy-completion story. The viewer can feel emotional dishonesty.

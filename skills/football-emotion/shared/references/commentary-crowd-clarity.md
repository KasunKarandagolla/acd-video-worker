# Commentary and Crowd Clarity

Crowd noise and commentary often fight in the same frequency area. Use practical repair before complex source separation.

## Practical order

1. Duck music under commentary first.
2. EQ music and non-speech ambience down around 1–4 kHz when commentary is active.
3. High-pass non-speech ambience where possible.
4. Use sidechain compression before advanced separation.
5. Use spectral separation only if OpenMontage/audio tools confirm support.

## Do not overclaim

Do not say the mix is clear until a listening pass or tool-assisted check confirms it.

# Football Playbook — Defending Champion Under Siege

Use when a champion or favorite is expected to win but nearly collapses.

## Structure

```text
Champion expectation → vulnerability exposed → pressure grows → survival moment → vindication → legacy question
```

## Mandatory clips

- Champion expectation / legacy burden.
- Opponent pressure proving the match is not easy.
- Champion visibly shaken: bench silence, missed chance, face close-up, crowd anxiety.
- Tactical or emotional turning point.
- Survival or decisive moment.
- Relief, not just celebration.

## Tone

This is not a pure underdog arc. The emotional payoff is **relief plus vindication**, not surprise alone.

## Useful titles

- `They were champions. Then they almost broke.`
- `The win that felt like survival.`
- `The hardest victory nobody expected.`

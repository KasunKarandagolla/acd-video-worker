# Priority Story Targets (reference for football-source-discovery)

Use these ten before chasing random viral clips. Each entry: why it's useful, and what to look
for. None of the specifics below (exact timestamps, view counts) are verified — treat everything
here as a search starting point, not a confirmed fact. Cross-check against
`shared/references/verification-standard.md` before using any of it in a downstream artifact.

## 1. Messi 2022 World Cup legacy completion
Emotion types: legacy, last chance, national pride, pressure, comeback. Deep-analysis priority: very high.
Look for: past-failure flashbacks, determined face close-ups, Argentina crowd shots, pressure
moments before decisive actions, final/trophy/family celebration.
Editor lesson: the story is not "Messi scored goals" — it's "a 15-year burden finally ended."

## 2. Argentina vs France 2022 World Cup final
Emotion types: pressure, rivalry, climax, heroic failure, legacy. Deep-analysis priority: very high.
Look for: 2-0 control phase, France comeback shock, Messi/Mbappé reaction contrast, penalty
shootout pressure, final emotional release.
Editor lesson: this is a multi-reversal story — build tension waves, not a flat match recap.

## 3. Ronaldo Portugal vs Morocco 2022 heartbreak
Emotion types: heartbreak, last chance, legacy. Deep-analysis priority: very high.
Look for: bench/entry emotion, final whistle reaction, tunnel walk, crying close-up, teammates/fans,
short career flashbacks only if they add meaning.
Editor lesson: a short real event can carry a whole video when supported by past-pressure and
final-chance framing, with respectful silence.

## 4. Brazil vs Croatia 2022 / Neymar heartbreak
Emotion types: heartbreak, pressure, heroic failure, national burden. Deep-analysis priority: high.
Look for: Neymar goal/high point, false-victory belief, Croatia equalizer/penalty tension, tears
and team collapse.
Editor lesson: use false victory as the emotional knife — the higher the hope, the deeper the heartbreak.

## 5. Germany 7-1 Brazil 2014
Emotion types: humiliation, national collapse, shock, trauma. Deep-analysis priority: high.
Look for: pre-match national expectation, fast collapse sequence, fans crying, stunned player
faces, scoreboard escalation, silence/aftermath.
Editor lesson: humiliation stories need restraint — do not edit tragedy like a hype reel.

## 6. Ghana vs Uruguay 2010
Emotion types: heartbreak, injustice, pressure, penalty tragedy. Deep-analysis priority: high.
Look for: late handball/chaos, red card, penalty setup, the miss, player/fan reaction, shootout collapse.
Editor lesson: penalty stories are built around waiting, not kicking — the pause before the kick is
the emotional center.

## 7. Roberto Baggio 1994 penalty miss
Emotion types: heartbreak, legacy, final chance, silence. Deep-analysis priority: high.
Look for: walk-up, the miss, body posture after, Brazil celebration contrast, Italian disappointment.
Editor lesson: one still body shape can be stronger than 20 action clips — hold it.

## 8. Morocco 2022 underdog run
Emotion types: underdog, national pride, shock, collective emotion. Deep-analysis priority: high.
Look for: team unity, fans/national pride, defensive suffering, final whistle release,
family/mother celebration when available.
Editor lesson: underdog videos need collective emotion, not just star-player emotion.

## 9. Mbappé 2022 final heroic failure
Emotion types: heroic failure, pressure, brilliance in defeat. Deep-analysis priority: high.
Look for: France struggling, Mbappé response goals, penalty/final effort, lonely defeat after brilliance.
Editor lesson: strongest line is "he did everything, but it was still not enough."

## 10. Iconic skill as emotional proof
Emotion types: iconic skill, proof, legacy, dominance. Deep-analysis priority: medium-high.
Examples: Messi vs Croatia 2022 assist against Gvardiol; iconic dribble moments — only when
connected to pressure/story, never as a standalone compilation.

## Search query patterns by category

- Story: `[player] [tournament/year] full journey`, `[team A] [team B] [year] emotional story`
- Moment: `[player] [event] emotional`, `[player] crying [context]`
- Editing-style: `football cinematic edit emotional`, `football motivational edit World Cup`
- Official-source: `FIFA [teams] [year] highlights`
- Non-English expansion: same query with player/match names + emotion keywords in Spanish, Arabic,
  Portuguese, French, Hindi, Tamil, Sinhala as relevant to the topic

## Rejected/high-risk categories (treat with extra caution)

- Any current-year World Cup claim not independently verified this session
- AI-generated football videos when the target is real emotional storytelling
- Shorts used as full story references (hook references only, not source footage)
- Fan edits with heavy copyrighted music or heavy watermarks
- Existing emotional compilations — reusing one leads straight to reused-content risk
